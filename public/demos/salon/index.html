FAQ

Before your visit

Do I need a patch test for colour?

Yes, for first-time colour clients, at least 48 hours before your appointment. It's free and takes five minutes.

What's your cancellation policy?

Please give 24 hours' notice. Late cancellations may be charged 50% of the service.

Do you do curly hair?

Yes! Two of our stylists are trained in curly cutting techniques.

Is there parking?

Two-hour free street parking right outside, and a public car park around the corner.
